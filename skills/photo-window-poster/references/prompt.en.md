# Photo Window Poster · Prompt (English-title version)

Prompt author: XXD (小小东). Append the per-image decisions (subject, window direction, boundary crossing, window colour, title copy) at the end.

```text
Turn each uploaded photograph into its own independent premium design poster — one poster per photo, no multi-image collage. Use a 3:4 portrait canvas split into an upper and a lower region of strictly equal height, 50% each.

Upper half: keep the original photograph. Preserve the subject's structure, real texture, natural light and the original colour atmosphere; apply only a light, high-end photographic grade so it reads like luxury brand advertising, an art magazine or an exhibition visual. To fit the frame you may naturally extend sky, ground or environment, but the subject itself stays unchanged.

Lower half: extract the photo's most recognisable subject, silhouette, pose and narrative relationship and rebuild them as a minimalist visual poster with premium commercial taste. The core language is "a real object passing through a geometric emotional window": place one narrow, restrained, low-saturation geometric colour block as the visual anchor and spatial container. Its direction is not fixed — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — decided naturally by the subject's centre of gravity, direction of movement, silhouette flow and page rhythm. The subject enters the block with real photographic or refined realistic quality and partially crosses, breaks, extends beyond or passes through its edge, creating a sense of natural growth, spatial penetration and breathing whitespace.

Keep the composition minimal, clear and balanced, ordered by the geometric block, the subject's boundary crossing and generous negative space. The subject may be centred, off-centre, growing vertically or spreading horizontally, but there is always a single focal point. Background in white, light grey or a very pale warm tone; optionally add extremely subtle cultural patterns, line graphics, contour lines, water lines, light silhouettes or abstract marks as a barely-there secondary layer.

Derive the palette from the most distinctive and lively colour relationships in the photo above, translated into a refined system of pale geometric window + true subject colour + minimal neutral typography. The block colour may be softened to mist blue, pale teal, off-white, pale pink, warm grey, pale gold or another light tone consistent with the source — light, clean, modern, refined overall.

Typography enters as part of the layout structure. Distil one short English title from the photo's theme, emotion, action, state or symbolism, with a small amount of subtitle, place, status word or micro-annotation. The title may run horizontally, vertically or along the relationship between block and subject; supporting text is set small, light-weight and generously letter-spaced, in the editorial manner of high-end real estate, luxury goods, aesthetic magazines or seasonal visuals.

The overall mood emphasises Eastern whitespace, modern order, natural vitality and premium commercial beauty. Whether the subject is a person, animal, plant, building, object, food, vehicle or landscape, maintain a clear, elegant and transferable correspondence between real photography and the geometric emotional window.
```
